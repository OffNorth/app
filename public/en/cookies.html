Delete data from your device

Withdrawing your consent prevents any further loading of TradingView. Data already stored on your device by TradingView or by the website can be deleted from your browser settings: for example, “Clear browsing data” in Chrome (Android), or Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data on iPhone.
