Without a choice from you, no TradingView chart is loaded. Your choice, whether acceptance or refusal, is kept for 6 months on your device and you will then be asked again. Simply browsing the website never counts as consent.

What the website stores

The website sets no cookies. It only uses your browser’s local storage, for purposes strictly necessary for the service you request. These uses are exempt from consent:

NamePurposeDuration
marches:crypto, marches:indices, marches:stocksLatest market data, for instant and offline displayUntil the next update or deletion
marches:tab, marches:langRemember the tab and language displayedUntil deletion
marches:installDismissedStop showing the app install hintUntil deletion
marches:consentRemember your TradingView choice (date, version, choice), which also serves as proof of that choice6 months
Service worker cache (marches-*)Operation of the installed app and offline mode (pages, styles, logos, latest data)Until the next app version or deletion

Delete data from your device

Withdrawing your consent prevents any further loading of TradingView. Data already stored on your device by TradingView or by the website can be deleted from your browser settings: for example, “Clear browsing data” in Chrome (Android), or Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data on iPhone.
