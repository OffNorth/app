Nature of the service

Markets is a free web application providing information on financial markets (cryptocurrencies, stock market indices, large listed companies). It offers no user account, no sales, no advertising and no investment service.

The information displayed is provided for information purposes only. It does not constitute investment advice, a recommendation to buy or sell, or a solicitation. Index and stock prices are delayed and may be incomplete or inaccurate.
