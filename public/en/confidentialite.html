Privacy policy

Information provided pursuant to Articles 12 to 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and French Law No. 78-17 of 6 January 1978 (“Informatique et Libertés”).

Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short

  • No account, no form, no advertising, no audience measurement: the website does not ask you for any personal data.
  • The website sets no cookies.
  • Market data and logos are fetched by our server: your IP address is not sent to the data providers or to the websites the logos come from.
  • Only the TradingView chart, if you accept it, sends data to a third party. Without your consent, it is not loaded.

Data controller

The data controller is the website publisher (see the legal notice). The publisher is not required to appoint a data protection officer (DPO) under Article 37 GDPR.

Recipients

  • Vercel Inc. (host), acting as the publisher’s processor for hosting. Vercel may also process certain technical data for its own purposes, in accordance with its privacy notice.
  • TradingView, only if you accept the display of charts, acting as an independent controller (TradingView policy).

No data is sold, rented or transferred for commercial or advertising purposes.

Transfers outside the European Union

  • Vercel Inc. is established in the United States. It states that it participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission of 10 July 2023 (Art. 45 GDPR).
  • TradingView (if you accept it) is established in particular in the United States; the safeguards applicable to its transfers are described in its privacy policy.

Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and portability, as well as the right to withdraw your consent at any time and to set instructions regarding your data after your death (Art. 15 to 22 GDPR, Art. 85 French Data Protection Act).

To exercise them, send your request to the website publisher. You will receive an answer within one month, which may be extended by two months if necessary (Art. 12 GDPR). As the website manages no accounts, the publisher generally holds no data that would allow you to be identified (Art. 11 GDPR).

You can withdraw your consent to TradingView in one click on the Cookie settings page. Data stored on your device can be deleted from your browser settings (site data).

Security

The website is served exclusively over HTTPS (HSTS enabled), with a restrictive content security policy (CSP). It has no forms, accounts or passwords. The website’s APIs are read-only, receive no personal data and cannot be used from third-party websites.

Minors

The website collects no data requiring parental consent. The display of TradingView charts, which requires consent, is intended for persons aged 15 or over, or authorised by the holder of parental authority (Art. 45 French Data Protection Act).
