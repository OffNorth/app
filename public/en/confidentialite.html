Privacy policy

Information provided pursuant to Articles 12 to 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and French Law No. 78-17 of 6 January 1978 (“Informatique et Libertés”).

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short

  • No account, no registration, no advertising, no audience measurement: the website does not ask for your name, email address or any identity data.
  • The website sets no cookies. Your favorites, settings and demo account stay on your device, unless you turn on demo account sync (details).
  • Market data and logos are fetched by our server: your IP address is not sent to the data providers or to the websites the logos come from.
  • The TradingView chart, if you accept it, sends data to a third party. Without your consent, it is not loaded.
  • If you create a price alert, your browser’s notification subscription address and your alerts are stored on a server located in the European Union, solely to notify you. You can delete everything at any time (details).

The data controller is the website publisher (see the legal notice). The publisher is not required to appoint a data protection officer (DPO) under Article 37 GDPR.

The publisher carries out no automated decision-making and no profiling. Market data (prices, indices, rankings) is not personal data.

Price alerts

Alerts are optional: no data is sent to the alert server until you create one. When you create your first alert, your browser asks for permission to show notifications, then creates a subscription address specific to the website; this address, the associated encryption keys and your alerts are stored so that the server can check prices (about every 5 minutes) and notify you.

An alert fires once: it is switched off after the notification is sent. Delivery is not guaranteed (sources sometimes delayed, browser push service); an alert is never financial advice.

Synced demo account and leaderboard

Sync is optional: without it, the demo account stays only on your device. If you turn it on, your device generates a random secret code; the server keeps only its fingerprint (SHA-256) together with the account content (play money). The server then executes your orders, TP and SL about every 5 minutes, even when the app is closed, with the same rules as the app; if you asked for it, it notifies you (see alerts).

Anyone who knows the code can view and change the account: keep it to yourself. No identity data is requested; the nickname is optional and visible to members of the groups you join, together with your performance (%) and number of trades: choose a nickname that does not identify you. The leaderboard is just for fun.

You can stop sync (the account stays on the server and can be recovered with the code) or permanently delete the synced account from the Account tab. An account unused for 1 year is deleted automatically.

Recipients

  • Vercel Inc. (host), acting as the publisher’s processor for hosting. Vercel may also process certain technical data for its own purposes, in accordance with its privacy notice.
  • Supabase Pte. Ltd., acting as the publisher’s processor, to store and check price alerts and synced demo accounts (data hosted in the European Union, Paris region). See its data processing agreement and its privacy policy.
  • Your browser’s push service (for example Google for Chrome and Android, Apple for Safari and iPhone, Mozilla for Firefox, Microsoft for Edge), only if you create an alert: it delivers the notification to your device. Its content is end-to-end encrypted (RFC 8291): the push service cannot read it.
  • TradingView, only if you accept the display of charts, acting as an independent controller (TradingView policy).

No data is sold, rented or transferred for commercial or advertising purposes.

Transfers outside the European Union

  • Vercel Inc. is established in the United States. It states that it participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission of 10 July 2023 (Art. 45 GDPR).
  • TradingView (if you accept it) is established in particular in the United States; the safeguards applicable to its transfers are described in its privacy policy.
  • Supabase Pte. Ltd. is established in Singapore. Alert and synced account data is hosted in the European Union; for transfers outside the Union, its data processing agreement provides for the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses (Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914, Art. 46 GDPR).
  • Browser push services may be located outside the European Union; they only receive the subscription address and encrypted notifications.

Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and portability, as well as the right to withdraw your consent at any time and to set instructions regarding your data after your death (Art. 15 to 22 GDPR, Art. 85 French Data Protection Act).

To exercise them, send your request to the website publisher. You will receive an answer within one month, which may be extended by two months if necessary (Art. 12 GDPR). As the website manages no accounts, the publisher generally holds no data that would allow you to be identified (Art. 11 GDPR).

For your price alerts, you can view and delete them yourself at any time in the Favorites tab, or delete everything (alerts and device) with the “Delete everything and turn off notifications” button. You can also revoke notification permission in your browser settings.

You can withdraw your consent to TradingView in one click on the Cookie settings page. Data stored on your device can be deleted from your browser settings (site data).

Security

The website is served exclusively over HTTPS (HSTS enabled), with a restrictive content security policy (CSP). It has no accounts or passwords. The website’s APIs are read-only, except for the price alert and synced demo account relay, and cannot be used from third-party websites. For alerts, the device is recognised by a random identifier of which only a fingerprint is kept on the server; notifications are end-to-end encrypted; the service’s secrets are kept in an encrypted vault (Supabase Vault).

Minors

The website collects no data requiring parental consent. The display of TradingView charts, which requires consent, is intended for persons aged 15 or over, or authorised by the holder of parental authority (Art. 45 French Data Protection Act).
